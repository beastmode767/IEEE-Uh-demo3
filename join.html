Become a Member

  • Exclusive networking with top corporate engineering sponsors.
  • Access to technical workshops and resume reviews.
  • Hands-on experience in R&D and hardware project teams.
  • Free access to the IEEE tutoring lounge.

3 Steps to Join

  1. Pay Local Dues: Submit your UH chapter dues via our portal.
  2. Register Nationally: Sign up on the official IEEE website to get your national member number.
  3. Connect: Join our Discord server to stay updated on all activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to be an Electrical Engineering major?

No! We welcome Computer Engineers, Mechanical Engineers, Computer Scientists, and any student passionate about technology.

What is the time commitment?

You can be as involved as you want. Project teams typically meet 2-4 hours a week, while general members can just attend workshops and socials.
